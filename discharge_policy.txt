
HOSPITAL DISCHARGE POLICY

Patients can be discharged only after the attending physician confirms
that the patient is medically stable.

Before discharge, hospital staff must verify:
1. Doctor approval
2. Final billing status
3. Medication instructions
4. Follow-up appointment
5. Discharge summary

The patient should receive a copy of the discharge summary.

Patients should be informed about their medications and follow-up
instructions before leaving the hospital.
