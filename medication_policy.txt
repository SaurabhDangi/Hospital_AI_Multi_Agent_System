
HOSPITAL MEDICATION POLICY

Medications should be administered only according to the physician's
orders.

Hospital staff must verify the patient's identity before administering
medication.

Staff should verify:
1. Correct patient
2. Correct medication
3. Correct dosage
4. Correct route
5. Correct timing

Medication administration should be documented in the patient's
medical record.

Any suspected medication error should be reported immediately.
